# Baltimore Ravens Statistics and Performance Report

## Executive Summary
This report aims to provide an in-depth analysis of the Baltimore Ravens' performance based on their statistical data.

## Offense
### Key Statistics
- **Passing Performance**: The team averaged 2.5 sacks allowed per game in 2023.
- **Wide Receiver Performance**: With a team-low grade of 68.9 in the receiving category, upgrading their wide receiver group is essential to expanding the team's passing capabilities.

## Defense
### Key Statistics
- **Linebacker Performance**: Patrick Queen (Grade: 73.5) and other Ravens linebackers underperformed in the coverage category.
- **Pass Protection**: The data suggests that the team could benefit from bolstering their pass protection.

## Key Findings and Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following areas should be prioritized for improvement:

* **Offensive Line**: Greg Roman (Grade: 72.2) needs support, particularly in pass protection.
* **Middle Linebacker**: Upgrade the linebacker position to enhance overall defensive effectiveness.
* **Wide Receiver (WR)**: Upgrade the wide receiver group to expand the team's passing capabilities.
* **Safety**: Adding a top-tier safety could elevate the team's defensive capabilities and depth.

## Conclusion
By focusing on addressing these areas, the Baltimore Ravens can improve their overall team performance and increase their chances of success in future seasons.
