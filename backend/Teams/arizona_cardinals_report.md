4. **Cornerback**: Defensive coverage has been a concern, particularly against deep passes. Look to draft a cornerback with exceptional speed, agility, and ball skills to improve this area.
5. **Safeties**: Defensive performance in the secondary has been a concern, particularly at safety. Look to draft a safety with exceptional tackling skills, speed, and range to improve this area.
